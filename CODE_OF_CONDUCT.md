# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Talaash aims to be a respectful and welcoming community for people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help keep discussion constructive and accessible.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and consider different perspectives and needs.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about work and ideas.
- Accept feedback in good faith, acknowledge mistakes, and correct them.
- Respect privacy and ask permission before sharing another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for others to participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated unwelcome contact and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, code reviews, project discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting concerns

Send concerns privately to [aalam.workacc@gmail.com](mailto:aalam.workacc@gmail.com). Include links or other relevant context, what happened, and how you would prefer to be contacted. Do not post sensitive evidence or personal information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as reasonably possible. Details may be shared with people who need them to assess or address the concern. If the report involves the maintainer, you may also use the hosting platform's reporting tools.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, and pattern of behavior. Responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content, a temporary participation restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may result in immediate restrictions.

Maintainers are expected to apply this policy fairly. Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through the private reporting address and provide additional context.
